## What to prepare before travelling

Neutral lightweight clothing, a hat, sun protection, insect repellent and closed or secure footwear are useful for wildlife days. A compact pair of binoculars can materially improve the experience. Keep cameras protected from dust and rain, and avoid packing around a promise to see one particular species because wildlife sightings remain unpredictable.

Share mobility requirements before booking because safari jeeps and uneven ground can be more demanding than ordinary sightseeing. Medication, insurance and emergency information should be carried offline, especially on longer park and nature sectors.

## Responsible travel on this route

Wildlife viewing should never depend on crowding animals, blocking movement or asking drivers to break park rules. Vacatina should work with operators that follow official access rules and allow sightings to happen at a respectful distance. Noise, feeding and off-route driving should not be treated as acceptable shortcuts to a better photograph.

The route should also spread spending beyond park gates where practical, using local guides, accommodation and food businesses that contribute to the surrounding communities. Conservation claims should be specific and verifiable rather than used as generic marketing language.
