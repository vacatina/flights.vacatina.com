Trip Overview

Escape to Bali for ten unforgettable days exploring lush jungles, volcanic landscapes, crystal-clear beaches, and centuries-old temples. Experience authentic Balinese hospitality while enjoying luxury accommodations, delicious local cuisine, and exciting outdoor adventures.

From sunrise volcano hikes to peaceful temple visits and island-hopping excursions, every day blends relaxation with discovery. Whether you're chasing waterfalls, snorkeling colorful reefs, or enjoying breathtaking sunsets, this journey showcases the very best of Bali.

Trip Highlights

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Welcome Bali

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport where your guide welcomes you with traditional Balinese flowers. Transfer to your beachfront resort and enjoy a relaxing afternoon after your flight.
