## What to prepare before travelling

Bring lightweight clothing, strong sun protection, swimwear, sandals and a small dry or day bag for coastal sectors. Reef-safe sun protection is preferable for marine days, and travellers planning surf or snorkelling should confirm whether equipment is included or rented locally. A light layer remains useful for air-conditioned transfers and any inland or early-morning sectors.

Keep flight details, insurance and booking confirmations available offline. Because this route combines several coasts and specialist activities, share swimming ability, dietary needs and any mobility limitations before the quotation is finalised so suitable hotels and operators can be checked.

## Responsible travel on this route

Marine experiences should only run with operators that follow local safety requirements and respect wildlife and reef environments. Sea turtles, dolphins, whales and other wildlife should never be pressured for photographs, touched or approached beyond operator guidance. Beach and village stops are more valuable when spending supports local businesses rather than treating them as quick photo calls.

At inland heritage or wildlife stops, the same principle applies: follow dress rules, park regulations and local instructions even when that changes the ideal photo or timetable. The itinerary is designed to protect the quality of the experience rather than force every optional activity in poor conditions.
