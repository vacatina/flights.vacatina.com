## Responsible travel on this route

Family travel works best when animal encounters, community visits and heritage sites are approached with the same local rules as adult tours. Children should be encouraged to observe wildlife without chasing or feeding animals, and temple dress and behaviour guidance should be explained before arrival.

The route should favour locally run experiences that can explain what visitors are participating in rather than using communities as scenery. Flexibility is part of responsible family travel because it avoids pushing tired travellers through activities simply to complete a checklist.
