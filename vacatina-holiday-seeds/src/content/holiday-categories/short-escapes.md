# Sri Lanka Short Escapes

Short, well-paced Sri Lanka journeys adapted from the five products in the Walkers “Chalo Colombo, Haalo Sri Lanka” collection.

## Tours in this collection

- **Colombo City Escape** — 3 days / 2 nights
- **Island Highlights Escape** — 5 days / 4 nights
- **Sri Lanka Extended Discovery** — 7 days / 6 nights
- **Sri Lanka Grand Circuit** — 8 days / 7 nights
- **Ultimate Island Journey** — 10 days / 9 nights

Every itinerary is enquiry-led and can be adjusted around dates, hotel level, pace and confirmed operating conditions.
