## What to prepare before travelling

The hill country can be cooler and wetter than the lowlands, so carry a light warm layer and rain protection alongside breathable daytime clothing. Comfortable shoes with grip are useful for viewpoints, gardens and trails. If a rail sector is important, travel with manageable luggage because station access and boarding are less convenient than a private vehicle transfer.

Keep booking documents offline and share mobility requirements early; steps, slopes and uneven paths are common around hill-country attractions and older properties.

## Responsible travel on this route

Tea estates, trails and viewpoints are working landscapes as well as visitor attractions. Travellers should stay on permitted routes, follow guide instructions and avoid entering operational areas without permission. Community and estate experiences should explain the people and work behind the landscape rather than reducing them to scenery.

At religious and heritage stops, local dress and photography rules remain part of the itinerary. Weather-related changes to walks or viewpoints should be accepted when safety or visibility is poor.
